MEDINOVA AI — EXPERIMENT 2C SUBSET-CONTROLLED ANALYSIS
========================================================================

Matched documents: 328
Control variable: document subset
Subsets: handwriting, mixed, normal, poor, rotated, tables

Subset-controlled Pearson correlations were calculated by residualizing both variables against subset dummy variables.
Subset-controlled Spearman correlations were calculated by ranking both variables first and then residualizing the ranks against subset dummy variables.
For controlled correlation p-values, the residual degrees of freedom were calculated as N - k - 2, where k is the number of independent subset dummy variables.

The analysis tests whether the observed association between OCR error and clinical information preservation remains after accounting for document subset.
It does not establish causality.
